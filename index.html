Md Farabi Mahbub

I am a research assistant in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at North South University, working on machine learning and deep learning. My interest is in segmentation and detection, modelling under class imbalance and data scarcity, and interpretability. My current project estimates groundwater contamination risk in Bangladesh from satellite imagery, terrain data and geological survey records, so that a site can be screened without a laboratory test.

I am currently looking for MSc and PhD opportunities in machine learning.

Current work

2026 onward

Groundwater contamination risk estimated from satellite data

Contamination risk predicted from satellite imagery, terrain and hydrological modelling, and national geological survey data, using remotely sensed signals with no water sample required. A public web application is being built alongside the models so that field workers without laboratory access can screen a site. Supervised by Dr. Ahsanur Rahman. Research

Groundwater contamination risk estimated from satellite data

Tens of millions of people in Bangladesh draw water from wells whose chemistry is unknown until somebody pays for a laboratory test. This project estimates contamination risk from satellite imagery, terrain and hydrological modelling, and national geological survey data, using remotely sensed signals with no sample required. A public web application is being built alongside the models so that communities and field workers without laboratory access can screen a site. Supervised by Dr. Ahsanur Rahman, ECE, North South University.

Publications

2024

Prediction of Glycemic Control in Diabetes Mellitus Patients Using Machine Learning.
Md. Farabi Mahbub, Warsi Omrao Khan Shuvo and Sifat Momen. Data Analytics in System Engineering: Proceedings of the 7th Computational Methods in Systems and Software 2023, Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, vol. 4, pages 216 to 242. Springer, Cham, 2024.

Supervised by Dr. Sifat Momen, ECE, North South University.

Abstract

While machine learning has made significant strides in diabetes prediction, glycemic control, a crucial aspect of diabetes management, remains understudied and calls for enhanced forecasting techniques. In addition to economic benefits, proper glycemic control also functions as a preventative measure against potential health complications. This paper aims to provide an accurate and reliable approach to predicting glycemic control in individuals afflicted with diabetes mellitus using advanced machine learning techniques. A vast and comprehensive dataset comprising 77,724 recently diagnosed diabetes patients from Istanbul province of Turkey in the year 2017 has been used in this study. Redundant features were eliminated and class imbalance was mitigated through the implementation of various sampling techniques. A collection of nine machine learning algorithms were utilized in order to predict glycemic control. Among the various trained models, LightGBM and CatBoost demonstrated exceptional performance, outperforming all other models with accuracy and AUC values of 83.36% and 89.49%, and 83.13% and 89.24% respectively. Explainable AI tools, such as LIME and SHAP, were employed to comprehend the predictions of the models and gain insights into important features.

Waste field detection from multi-source satellite imagery

A deep learning framework for locating waste disposal sites in remote sensing data. Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical composites were generated and stacked into multi-channel inputs. Cross-validation, focal loss and ensemble classification handled a target class occupying a very small fraction of pixels. Carried out in the Data and Innovation Research Programme at the BRAC Institute of Governance and Development, under Dr. Munshi Sulaiman and Tahmid Bin Mahmud.

Generative models under data scarcity

Machine learning on environmental and geological data to assess groundwater contamination risk, with conditional GANs used to hold performance up when the training set is smaller than the problem requires. Supervised by Dr. Ahsanur Rahman, ECE, North South University.

2022

Identifying and quantifying visual pollution

Detecting and counting visual pollution in urban environments from street-level imagery. I compiled a labelled dataset from Google Street View and augmented it for training, then trained and compared several YOLO detectors on it. A prototype runs the best of them on video from a moving vehicle, tracking polluted objects and summarising what it finds along a route. Supervised by Dr. Shahnewaz Siddique, ECE, North South University.

2022

Coin U-Net: context invariance in image segmentation

Biomedical image segmentation where a plain U-Net loses context. Coin U-Net adds a Siamese branch that trains on each image paired with a masked version, which gives the model a context signal for separating infected regions from healthy tissue and captures both short and long-range structure. Supervised by Dr. Nabeel Mohammed and Dr. Hasib Zunair, ECE, North South University.

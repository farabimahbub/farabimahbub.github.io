SCIKIT-LEARNCGANDJANGO

From Data to Safety: arsenic prediction before digging

A machine learning framework that predicts groundwater arsenic from parameters available before a well is dug, removing the need for costly water sampling. CGAN-synthesised data covers gaps in the record, explainable AI keeps the predictions inspectable, and the best model was deployed as a web application.

PYTORCHYOLOv7FLASK

Counting visual pollution from a moving car

A labelled street-level dataset compiled from Google Street View and augmented for training, several YOLO detectors trained on it, and a prototype that runs YOLOv7 on dashcam footage in real time, tracking and counting polluted objects into a dashboard summarising pollution along a route.

PYTORCHSIAMESEU-NET

Coin U-Net: segmentation with a context signal

Biomedical segmentation where a plain U-Net struggles. A Siamese branch trains on each image paired with a masked version, giving the model a context signal for separating infected regions from healthy tissue, and capturing both short and long-range structure.

SCIKIT-LEARNSHAP · LIMESMOTE

Glycemic control in diabetes patients

Predicting whether a patient’s blood sugar is under control, with SMOTE for the class imbalance and SHAP, LIME and Shapash to show what the model was keying on. Published at CoMeSySo 2023 and listed under Research.

DJANGOSTRIPEJQUERY

FridayHaat: an online shop

An eCommerce platform built on Django, with a custom user model, email activation, Google sign-in through allauth, and guest checkout for buyers who want no account. Stripe handles customers, cards and charges. Product search runs across title, description, category, price, manufacturer and tags in a single query. The cart, the contact form and the payment step call a JSON endpoint through jQuery, so those actions leave the page in place.

Code available on request.
